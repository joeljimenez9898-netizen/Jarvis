# Northgate Hotel

Prospect, not yet a client. Ninety-room business hotel with a breakfast service and a lobby bar. Estimated volume of thirty-five kilos a week, which would make it our largest account and would push the current roaster close to capacity.

[[Sam Whitlock]] has had two tasting sessions with their food and beverage manager. They want a custom blend, staff training, and a machine maintenance arrangement. The quote has been run through the [[Pricing Model]] at a lower margin than usual on the basis of volume. Winning this account is the main reason the [[Second Roaster Purchase]] is being considered now rather than next year.

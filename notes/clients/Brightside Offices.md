# Brightside Offices

Office coffee client: a co-working building with about two hundred members. Takes twenty kilos a month of the house blend for bean-to-cup machines. Good volume, low effort to serve.

The problem is payment. Invoices have been paid between thirty and fifty days late for three consecutive months, which prompted [[Priya Nair]] to draft the [[Late Payment Policy]]. Their facilities manager changed in July and the new one did not know we existed. A call is planned to reset terms. If the account is lost, the [[Q3 2026 Financial Review]] estimates a monthly revenue gap of roughly eleven hundred euros.

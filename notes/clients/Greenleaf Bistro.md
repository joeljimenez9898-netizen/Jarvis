Wholesale client since 2023. Vegetarian bistro on Canal Road, about forty covers. Orders twelve kilos a week, mostly the house espresso blend, plus a single-origin filter that rotates monthly. Pays on time, every time.

Account managed by [[Sam Whitlock]]. They asked for barista training for two new hires, which we are delivering through the [[Barista Training Program]] at cost. They are interested in co-branding a retail bag, which needs the new label design from the [[Rebrand Project]]. Price reviewed in the [[Pricing Model]] in August with a three percent increase accepted without pushback.

# The Copper Kettle

Independent cafe and bakery in the old market hall. Wholesale client since 2024, eight kilos a week. Owner is friendly but very price sensitive and compares us with two other roasters every quarter.

Sam Whitlock manages the account. They complained twice about inconsistent grind on pre-ground bags, which traced back to the old grinder and is one of the arguments in the [[Second Roaster Purchase]] proposal for a production grinder too. Delivered on the Friday round in [[Delivery Routes]]. Margin on this account is the thinnest of all wholesale clients according to the [[Q3 2026 Financial Review]].

# Saltmarsh Coffee Co

Small specialty coffee roastery and cafe founded by [[Maya Okafor]] in 2019. Five people including part-timers. Annual revenue around four hundred and twenty thousand euros, split roughly fifty percent cafe, thirty-five percent wholesale and fifteen percent online and the [[Subscription Box]].

Sells through the [[Harbour Street Cafe]], wholesale accounts such as [[Greenleaf Bistro]], [[The Copper Kettle]] and [[Brightside Offices]], and online. Main supplier is [[Finca La Esperanza]]. Big questions for the next six months: the [[Second Roaster Purchase]], winning [[Northgate Hotel]], and protecting margins after the [[Q3 2026 Financial Review]].

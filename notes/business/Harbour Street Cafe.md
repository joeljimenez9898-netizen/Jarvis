# Harbour Street Cafe

The flagship cafe of [[Saltmarsh Coffee Co]], opened in 2021 on Harbour Street. Twenty-eight seats inside, eight outside. Open seven thirty to five, six days a week, closed on Sundays. Average of two hundred and sixty transactions on a weekday.

Managed by [[Leo Brandt]]. Daily operations follow the [[Opening Checklist]] and [[Closing Checklist]]; new staff go through the [[Barista Training Program]]. The roaster is in the back room, which customers love and the ventilation does not. The [[Loyalty App Launch]] and the post-breakdown maintenance contract are the two current cafe projects.

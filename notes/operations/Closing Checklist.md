# Closing Checklist

For the last shift at [[Harbour Street Cafe]]. The cafe closes at five; the close takes about forty minutes.

Backflush the group heads with detergent, soak the portafilters and baskets, purge and wipe the steam wands, empty and clean the drip trays. Empty the grinder chute but not the hopper. Wipe down all surfaces, mop, take out recycling and compost. Cash up and record the till variance. After the [[Espresso Machine Breakdown]], descaling checks were added on Fridays. Owned by [[Leo Brandt]]. Pairs with the [[Opening Checklist]].

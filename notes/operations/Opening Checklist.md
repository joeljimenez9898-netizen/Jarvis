# Opening Checklist

For the first shift at [[Harbour Street Cafe]]. Arrive at six forty-five for a seven thirty open.

Turn on the espresso machine first, it needs twenty-five minutes to reach stable temperature. Check the grinder hopper and purge two shots. Dial in the house espresso: eighteen grams in, thirty-six out, twenty-eight to thirty-two seconds. Log the dial-in on the clipboard. Check milk fridge temperature is below four degrees. Count the float. Put out the pastry delivery and check it against the order. Owned by [[Leo Brandt]] and taught in week one of the [[Barista Training Program]]. Pairs with the [[Closing Checklist]].

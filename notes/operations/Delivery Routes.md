# Delivery Routes

Wholesale deliveries run twice a week in the van, planned by [[Sam Whitlock]].

Tuesday round: [[Greenleaf Bistro]], [[Brightside Offices]] and two smaller accounts in the north of town, about two and a half hours. Friday round: [[The Copper Kettle]] and the market hall stalls, about ninety minutes. Orders close at noon the day before so they can be roasted and rested overnight. If [[Northgate Hotel]] signs, it would need a third weekly drop on Thursdays. Fuel and van costs are tracked in the [[Pricing Model]] as a per-kilo delivery cost.

# Barista Training Program

Four-week programme for new baristas, also sold to wholesale clients such as [[Greenleaf Bistro]] at cost and offered to [[Northgate Hotel]] as part of their proposal.

Week one covers the [[Opening Checklist]], [[Closing Checklist]], hygiene and the till. Week two is espresso: dose, yield, time and dialling in. Week three is milk texture and latte art basics. Week four is filter brewing, customer service and a final assessment with [[Leo Brandt]]. Trainees shadow for at least six shifts before working solo. Materials are a printed handbook and short videos recorded in the cafe.

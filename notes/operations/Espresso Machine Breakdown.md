# Espresso Machine Breakdown

On 29 September the steam boiler on the cafe espresso machine at [[Harbour Street Cafe]] failed mid-morning. Pressure relief valve stuck open; boiler element burned out. The cafe ran on batch brew and one group for two days.

Repair cost seven hundred and eighty euros including a call-out on a Sunday. Estimated lost sales around nine hundred euros. Root cause was scale build-up; the machine had not been descaled since March. Actions: weekly descaling check added to the [[Closing Checklist]], and [[Leo Brandt]] is getting quotes for an annual maintenance contract. Discussed at the [[Weekly Ops Meeting]].

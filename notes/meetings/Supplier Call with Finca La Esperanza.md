# Supplier Call with Finca La Esperanza

Video call on 22 September with [[Elena Ruiz]] and [[Maya Okafor]]. Agenda: harvest outlook, shipping dates, and the anaerobic micro-lot.

Harvest is running three weeks late because of rain, so the main container will land in early December instead of mid November. That is a risk for the [[Holiday Blend Launch]]; the fallback is to use remaining stock from the previous crop for the base. The anaerobic lot is sixty kilos at a price premium of about forty percent. Elena asked for roast profiles to share with the farm. Follow-up captured in the [[Weekly Ops Meeting]].

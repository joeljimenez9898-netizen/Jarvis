# Q3 2026 Financial Review

Quarterly review led by [[Priya Nair]] on 3 October. Revenue for the quarter was one hundred and twelve thousand euros, up nine percent on Q2. Cafe revenue grew, wholesale was flat, and the [[Subscription Box]] grew but at a lower margin because of postage.

Gross margin on wholesale dropped from forty-one to thirty-seven percent, mainly green coffee prices and the discount given to [[The Copper Kettle]]. Overdue receivables are concentrated in [[Brightside Offices]]. Cash buffer is just under three months of costs. Recommendations: enforce the [[Late Payment Policy]], reprice using the [[Pricing Model]], and finance the [[Second Roaster Purchase]] with debt rather than cash.

# Northgate Hotel Tasting

Second tasting session at [[Northgate Hotel]] on 30 September with their food and beverage manager and head of breakfast. [[Sam Whitlock]] brought three blend options.

They preferred the medium roast with the Brazil base for breakfast and the house espresso for the lobby bar. Concerns raised: reliability of supply at thirty-five kilos a week, and who services the machines. Sam proposed training through the [[Barista Training Program]] and a quarterly machine check. Next step is a written proposal priced through the [[Pricing Model]] by mid October.

# Subscription Box

Monthly coffee subscription launched in February 2026. Two bags of single origin per month, shipped on the first Tuesday. Currently one hundred and forty subscribers with churn around six percent a month.

Packed by hand on Mondays. [[Sam Whitlock]] proposes packing on delivery days instead. Postage went up eleven percent in September, which the [[Q3 2026 Financial Review]] flagged as squeezing the margin. Ideas on the table: raise the price by one euro, offer a quarterly plan, and add a cafe discount tied to the [[Loyalty App Launch]]. December box will feature the [[Holiday Blend Launch]].

# Rebrand Project

Refresh of the logo, bag labels and cafe signage. The original logo was made in an afternoon in 2019 and does not print well on kraft bags. Working with a freelance designer on a fixed fee of three thousand two hundred euros.

Two rounds of concepts done; the team picked the tidal line mark. Remaining work is the label system for single origins and the [[Holiday Blend Launch]] packaging. Blocks the [[Loyalty App Launch]] artwork and the [[Greenleaf Bistro]] co-branded bag. [[Maya Okafor]] signs off on design. Expected completion is end of October.

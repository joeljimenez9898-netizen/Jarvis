# Second Roaster Purchase

Proposal to buy a fifteen kilo drum roaster to sit alongside the current five kilo machine. Quoted price is thirty-eight thousand euros installed, plus about four thousand for ventilation work.

Drivers: the current roaster runs six days a week already, [[Northgate Hotel]] would add thirty-five kilos weekly, and the [[Holiday Blend Launch]] creates a seasonal spike every December. [[Maya Okafor]] is in favour. [[Priya Nair]] wants it financed through the [[Bank Loan Application]] rather than cash, to keep a three month buffer. Decision deferred at the [[Weekly Ops Meeting]] on 6 October until the loan outcome is known.

# Holiday Blend Launch

Seasonal blend for December, sold in the cafe, online and through the [[Subscription Box]]. Last year it sold out in nine days, which left money on the table.

This year the base is the washed Caturra from [[Finca La Esperanza]], with a small anaerobic lot offered by [[Elena Ruiz]] for a fruit-forward finish. Target is four hundred bags, double last year, which is tight on the current roaster and is cited in the [[Second Roaster Purchase]] proposal. Packaging depends on the [[Rebrand Project]]. Tasting notes draft: dark cherry, cocoa, orange peel.

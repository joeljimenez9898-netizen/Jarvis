# Loyalty App Launch

Replace the paper stamp card at [[Harbour Street Cafe]] with a digital loyalty programme. Shortlisted two off-the-shelf apps that integrate with the till. Target launch is mid November, before the holiday trade.

[[Leo Brandt]] is the project owner. Rules agreed so far: one stamp per hot drink, tenth drink free, double stamps on Mondays. Open question is whether [[Subscription Box]] customers get a cafe discount. Cost is around forty euros a month. The brand assets needed for the app depend on the [[Rebrand Project]], which is the main risk to the date.

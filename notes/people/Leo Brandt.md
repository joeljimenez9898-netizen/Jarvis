Cafe manager at [[Harbour Street Cafe]] since March 2022. Runs the rota, trains baristas using the [[Barista Training Program]], and owns the [[Opening Checklist]] and [[Closing Checklist]]. Previously a shift lead at a chain, so he pushes for consistency and checklists over improvisation.

Leo raised the [[Espresso Machine Breakdown]] incident and wants a maintenance contract instead of ad hoc repairs. He is also the person most keen on the [[Loyalty App Launch]], because regulars keep asking for a stamp card that does not get lost. Reports to [[Maya Okafor]]. Attends the [[Weekly Ops Meeting]] every Monday.

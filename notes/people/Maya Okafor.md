# Maya Okafor

Founder and head roaster of [[Saltmarsh Coffee Co]]. Started roasting in a rented garage in 2019 and opened the [[Harbour Street Cafe]] in 2021. Owns the roast profiles, green coffee buying and anything that touches flavour. Prefers decisions written down rather than discussed in passing, which is why the [[Weekly Ops Meeting]] notes exist at all.

She is the main contact for [[Finca La Esperanza]] and personally negotiated the 2026 contract. Her current priorities are getting the [[Second Roaster Purchase]] approved and stabilising wholesale margins after the [[Q3 2026 Financial Review]] showed them slipping. She delegates cafe operations to [[Leo Brandt]] and books to [[Priya Nair]].

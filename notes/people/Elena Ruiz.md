# Elena Ruiz

Export manager at [[Finca La Esperanza]] in Huila, Colombia. Main supplier contact for the washed Caturra and the honey-processed Pink Bourbon lots. Communicates mostly by WhatsApp voice notes, in Spanish, and visits Europe once a year in May.

Elena agreed to the 2026 forward contract with [[Maya Okafor]] and has offered an early look at a small anaerobic lot that could anchor the [[Holiday Blend Launch]]. She asked for harvest photos and roast notes to be shared back so the farm can use them in its own marketing.

# Pricing Model

Spreadsheet built by [[Priya Nair]] that produces a per-kilo wholesale price for any blend or single origin.

Inputs: green coffee cost per kilo, roast loss of about sixteen percent, packaging, labour per kilo, energy, and delivery cost from [[Delivery Routes]]. Target gross margin is forty percent for standard accounts and thirty-two percent for high-volume accounts like [[Northgate Hotel]]. Floor price rule: no account below a thirty percent margin. The [[Q3 2026 Financial Review]] showed [[The Copper Kettle]] has drifted below the floor and needs repricing. Updated monthly with green coffee prices.

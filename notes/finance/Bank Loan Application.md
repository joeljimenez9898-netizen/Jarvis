# Bank Loan Application

Application submitted on 1 October for a forty-five thousand euro equipment loan over five years to fund the [[Second Roaster Purchase]] and ventilation work.

Supporting documents: two years of accounts, the [[Q3 2026 Financial Review]] pack, cash flow forecast and a letter of intent from [[Northgate Hotel]] if Sam can get one. The bank indicated a decision in three to four weeks and an interest rate around seven percent. [[Priya Nair]] modelled repayments at about nine hundred euros a month, which she considers affordable only if Northgate signs.

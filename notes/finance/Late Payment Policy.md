# Late Payment Policy

Drafted by [[Priya Nair]] in September after repeated late payments from [[Brightside Offices]].

Standard terms are fourteen days. A friendly reminder goes out at day fifteen, a phone call at day twenty-one, and deliveries pause at day thirty until the balance is cleared. Accounts that are late three times in six months move to payment on order. Statutory late payment interest is reserved for accounts that are over sixty days. The policy will be sent to all wholesale clients with October invoices, after sign-off from [[Maya Okafor]]. Recommended in the [[Q3 2026 Financial Review]].

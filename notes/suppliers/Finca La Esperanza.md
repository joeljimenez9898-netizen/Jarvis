# Finca La Esperanza

Family farm in Huila, Colombia, at seventeen hundred to nineteen hundred metres. Our most important green coffee supplier, about forty percent of annual volume. Direct trade relationship since 2022, contact [[Elena Ruiz]].

Lots we buy: washed Caturra as the backbone of the house espresso, and a honey Pink Bourbon for filter. 2026 contract is a forward price of seven euros eighty per kilo, roughly fifteen percent above commodity. Harvest delays this season are covered in the [[Supplier Call with Finca La Esperanza]]. Supplies the base for the [[Holiday Blend Launch]]. Relationship owned by [[Maya Okafor]].
